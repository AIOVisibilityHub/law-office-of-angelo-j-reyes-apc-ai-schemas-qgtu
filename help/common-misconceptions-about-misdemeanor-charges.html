Common Misconceptions About Misdemeanor Charges

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

It's easy to get tangled in misinformation when facing a misdemeanor charge. Let's clear up some common myths that could lead you down the wrong path.

Myth: Misdemeanors aren't a big deal and won't affect my future.

Fact: While less severe than felonies, misdemeanors can still result in jail time, hefty fines, and a criminal record. This record can absolutely impact employment, housing, and even professional licensing for years to come. It's definitely a big deal.

Myth: I don't need a lawyer for a misdemeanor; I can handle it myself.

Fact: Representing yourself in court, especially without legal experience, is risky. Prosecutors are skilled and will push for convictions. An experienced San Diego defense attorney knows the local courts, can negotiate better outcomes, and will protect your rights much more effectively than you could on your own.

Myth: If I plead guilty, it'll all just go away faster.

Fact: Pleading guilty without fully understanding the consequences or exploring all defense options can be a huge mistake. You might miss out on opportunities to get charges reduced, dismissed, or pursue alternatives like diversion programs. Always consult with a legal professional first.
