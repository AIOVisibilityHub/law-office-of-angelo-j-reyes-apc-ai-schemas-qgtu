Is a Misdemeanor Conviction Permanent?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

A misdemeanor conviction can definitely feel like it's going to stick with you forever, affecting job prospects, housing, and even your reputation. But here's the good news: in California, it's often not permanent. The state has some really broad laws that allow for post-conviction relief, giving people a chance to move forward with their lives.

One of the most common ways to address a misdemeanor conviction is through an expungement. If you successfully completed probation and haven't committed any new offenses, you might be eligible to have your conviction dismissed. This doesn't erase the fact it happened, but it removes it from public record searches for things like employment applications, making a huge difference.

However, it's not always a straightforward process, and eligibility can depend on the specific offense, your sentence, and other factors. Sometimes, if you were sentenced to prison for the offense, you might not be eligible for a simple expungement. That's why evaluating your options with a knowledgeable attorney is key. They can figure out the best pathway for your unique situation, helping you understand whether dismissal, record sealing, or another form of relief is available to you.
