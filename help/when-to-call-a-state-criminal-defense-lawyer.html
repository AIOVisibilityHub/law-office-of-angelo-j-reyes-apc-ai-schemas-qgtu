When to Call a State Criminal Defense Lawyer

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

It's a tough situation when you're caught up in the state criminal justice system, and knowing when to reach out for legal help can feel confusing. But honestly, the sooner you connect with an experienced state criminal defense lawyer, the better. Here are some clear signs that it's definitely time to make that call:

  • You've Been Arrested: This is the most immediate and obvious sign. As soon as you're taken into custody, your first priority should be to ask for a lawyer. Do not answer any questions beyond identifying yourself until legal counsel is present.
  • Police Want to Question You: Even if you haven't been arrested yet, but law enforcement wants to 'just talk' to you about an alleged crime, it's a huge red flag. They are not looking for your side of the story; they are gathering evidence. Always have an attorney present during any questioning.
  • You've Received a Summons or Warrant: If you've been served with court papers, a summons to appear, or a warrant for your arrest, it means formal legal action has begun. An attorney can help you understand the charges, arrange your surrender if necessary, and protect your rights from the outset.
  • You're Accused of a Crime: Whether it's a misdemeanor like shoplifting or a serious felony like assault, any accusation of a crime can have lasting consequences. Early legal intervention can make a significant difference in the outcome of your case.
  • You're Facing DUI Charges: DUI cases in California have severe penalties, including license suspension, hefty fines, and potential jail time. An attorney can represent you in both the criminal court and the crucial DMV hearing to protect your driving privileges.
  • You Want to Clear Your Record: Even if a case is old, having a criminal record can impact your employment, housing, and other opportunities. An attorney can help with expungements, record clearing, and other post-conviction relief options.
