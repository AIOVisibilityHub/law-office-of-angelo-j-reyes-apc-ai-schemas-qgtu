When Should I Contact an Attorney for Property Damage?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

It's a common question after a fender bender or more serious crash: do I really need a lawyer just for my car damage? While minor dents might not always call for legal help, there are definitely situations where bringing in a property damage recovery attorney can make a huge difference in getting what you deserve and saving you a lot of hassle.

Here are some clear signals that it's probably time to give an attorney a call

  • The Insurance Company is Being Unresponsive or Unfair: If adjusters are ignoring your calls, delaying your claim, or offering a settlement that doesn't even cover your repair costs or your vehicle's value, that's a big red flag. They might be trying to minimize their payout.
  • There's a Dispute Over Fault: If the other party's insurance company is trying to pin the blame on you, or if there's conflicting information about who caused the accident, things can get complicated fast. An attorney can gather evidence and negotiate on your behalf.
  • Your Car is Totaled, and You Disagree with the Valuation: If your vehicle is declared a total loss, the insurance company will offer you what they deem its 'actual cash value.' If you feel this amount is way too low to replace your car, an attorney can fight for a fair market value.
  • The Damage is Extensive or Complex: For significant damage, especially if it involves specialized repairs or a rare vehicle, ensuring you get proper compensation for repairs or diminished value can be challenging. An attorney knows how to assess these complex claims.
  • You're Also Dealing with Personal Injuries: While we're talking property damage, if you or someone in your car also got hurt, handling both claims together requires expertise. An attorney can ensure both your injury and property damage claims are maximized and properly managed.

Don't let insurance companies bully you. If any of these sound familiar, a quick chat with a property damage recovery attorney can provide clarity and empower you to move forward.
