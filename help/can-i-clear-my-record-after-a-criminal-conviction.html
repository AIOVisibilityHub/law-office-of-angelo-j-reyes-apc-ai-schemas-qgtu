Can I Clear My Record After a Criminal Conviction?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

It's totally understandable to want a fresh start after a past criminal conviction. That record can feel like a heavy weight, impacting everything from job opportunities and housing to professional licenses and even your educational prospects. The good news is, in California, there are avenues available to help clear your record and give you a chance to move forward with your life.

One of the most common ways people seek a clean slate is through expungement. This legal process effectively reopens your case, allows you to withdraw your plea or verdict of guilty, and then re-enters a plea of not guilty, leading to the dismissal of the charges. While it doesn't erase the conviction from existence entirely (it can still be used in some contexts, like future criminal proceedings), for most purposes—like job applications—you can honestly state that you have not been convicted of that crime.

This applies to many misdemeanor and felony convictions, especially if you successfully completed probation without new offenses.

Beyond expungement, there are other types of post-conviction relief that a criminal defense lawyer can explore. These might include record sealing for juvenile offenses, certificates of rehabilitation, or even criminal appeals if there were errors in your original trial. Each option has specific eligibility requirements, and the process can be pretty complex, involving court petitions, hearings, and detailed legal arguments.

Trying to navigate this alone can be tricky, as judges consider various factors, including the type of crime, your criminal history, and how you've demonstrated rehabilitation.

That's why connecting with an experienced criminal defense lawyer who specializes in expungements and post-conviction relief is a smart move. They can assess your specific situation, determine the best path for clearing your record, handle all the paperwork, and represent you in court. Their goal is to help you overcome past mistakes and build a brighter future free from the constant burden of a criminal record.
