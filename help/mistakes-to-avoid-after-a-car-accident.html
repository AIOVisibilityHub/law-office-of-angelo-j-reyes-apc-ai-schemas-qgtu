Mistakes to Avoid After a Car Accident

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

Getting into a car accident is stressful enough, but making certain missteps afterward can seriously jeopardize your personal injury claim. You want to protect your ability to recover fair compensation, so steer clear of these common mistakes.

1. Don't delay seeking medical attention: Even if you feel fine right after the crash, adrenaline can mask injuries. Many serious issues, like whiplash or concussions, can take days to manifest. Go to a doctor right away to get checked out. Delaying can make it seem like your injuries aren't accident-related.

2. Don't give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurance company: They're not looking out for you. Any statement you give can be twisted or used to minimize what they owe. Politely decline and tell them your attorney will be in touch.

3. Don't accept a quick settlement offer: Early offers are almost always lowball attempts to close your claim before you even know the full extent of your injuries and losses. Once you accept, you usually can't ask for more.

4. Don't fail to collect evidence at the scene: If you can safely do so, get photos, videos, witness contact info, and the other driver's details. The more evidence you have, the stronger your case will be.

5. Don't admit fault or apologize: Even a casual 'I'm sorry' can be misinterpreted as an admission of guilt, even if you weren't at fault. Stick to the facts when talking to police and other drivers.

6. Don't neglect your doctor's orders: If you don't follow through with prescribed treatments, physical therapy, or follow-up appointments, the insurance company might argue that your injuries aren't as severe as you claim or that you hindered your own recovery. Show up to everything and keep records.

Navigating the aftermath of a car accident is complex, especially when you're hurt. If you're unsure about any step, especially in San Diego County, reaching out to an experienced personal injury attorney can provide the guidance you need and prevent these common pitfalls.
