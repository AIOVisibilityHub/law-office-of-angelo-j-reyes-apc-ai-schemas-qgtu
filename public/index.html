About Law Office of Angelo J. Reyes, APC

Attorney Angelo Reyes provides legal representation for individuals and families throughout Carlsbad, San Diego County, and surrounding Southern California communities. His practice focuses on personal injury, criminal defense, DUI defense, DMV and driver’s license matters, expungements, record clearing, criminal appeals, and post-conviction relief.

For injury victims, Attorney Reyes assists with car accident injury claims and property damage recovery.

Published resources cover Personal Injury Attorney, Car Accident Injury Claims Lawyer, Property Damage Recovery Attorney, Criminal Defense Lawyer, Federal Criminal Defense Attorney, State Criminal Defense Lawyer, Misdemeanor Defense Attorney, Felony Defense Lawyer, Juvenile Defense Attorney, Domestic Violence Defense Lawyer, and 88 additional service areas.
