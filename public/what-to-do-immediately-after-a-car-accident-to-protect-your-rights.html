What to do immediately after a car accident to protect your rights

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

Car accidents are scary and disorienting, and it's easy to forget what to do in the chaos. But those first few moments can really impact your ability to get fair compensation later on. Taking the right steps immediately after a crash can make a huge difference in protecting your rights and future claim. Don't let the stress get to you; stay calm and focus on these key actions.

Here are some crucial steps to take

  • Ensure Safety: First and foremost, check for injuries and move to a safe location if possible. Your well-being is the priority.
  • Call 911: Always call the police and emergency services, even if the accident seems minor. A police report is vital documentation of the incident, including who was involved and initial observations of fault.
  • Exchange Information: Get the other driver's name, contact information, insurance details, and license plate number. Don't discuss fault at the scene.
  • Document Everything: Use your phone to take photos and videos of the accident scene, vehicle damage, road conditions, traffic signs, and any visible injuries. The more evidence, the better.
  • Seek Medical Attention: Even if you feel fine, see a doctor as soon as possible. Some injuries, like whiplash, can have delayed symptoms. Medical records directly link your injuries to the accident.
  • Notify Your Insurance: Inform your insurance company about the accident, but be careful what you say. Do not give recorded statements to the other driver's insurance company without legal advice.

Remember, insurance companies start working to minimize payouts almost immediately.
