What Should I Do After a Hit and Run Accident?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

Getting into any accident is jarring, but a hit and run can be especially confusing and scary. Your first steps after a hit and run in San Diego are really important, not just for your safety but also for your legal standing. It’s natural to feel panicked, but try to stay calm and focus on these immediate actions. Your actions right after the incident can significantly impact what happens next. Remember, leaving the scene can turn a stressful situation into a criminal charge, even if you weren't at fault for the original collision.

First, assess the situation for injuries. If anyone is hurt, including yourself, call 911 immediately. Even if you think the injuries are minor, medical attention should be a priority.

Next, if it’s safe to do so, move your vehicle to the side of the road to prevent further accidents. Then, gather as much information as you can about the other vehicle and driver, even if they're long gone. This includes their license plate number, make, model, color, and any distinguishing features like bumper stickers or damage.

Also, note the time, location, and direction of travel. Look for witnesses and ask for their contact information; their statements can be incredibly valuable.

Once you’ve taken care of safety and gathered initial information, you need to report the incident to the police. This is crucial for both insurance purposes and any potential legal action against the hit and run driver. Even if the damage is minor, file a report. Be sure to document the scene with photos and videos if possible, capturing vehicle damage, road conditions, and any relevant landmarks. These details help investigators and your insurance company. Remember, in California, leaving the scene of an accident where there's damage to another vehicle or property, or injuries, is a crime.

After reporting, contact your insurance company to inform them of the hit and run. They’ll guide you on the claims process. It's really smart to also get legal advice, even if you think it's a minor incident. A lawyer experienced in hit and run cases in San Diego can help you understand your rights, deal with insurance companies, and if the other driver is found, assist with criminal proceedings or civil claims. Don't try to navigate this alone; a free consultation can give you peace of mind and clear next steps.
