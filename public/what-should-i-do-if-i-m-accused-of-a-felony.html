What Should I Do If I'm Accused of a Felony?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

Being accused of a felony is incredibly serious, and it can turn your whole world upside down. The stakes are high, with potential prison time, hefty fines, and long-lasting impacts on your life. Taking the right steps immediately can make a significant difference in your outcome. Here's a quick guide to help you navigate this tough situation.

First and foremost, do not speak with law enforcement without your attorney present. Anything you say can be used against you, so politely but firmly assert your right to remain silent and to have legal counsel. Next, contact an experienced felony defense lawyer right away.

Time is critical in these cases, as evidence needs to be gathered and a defense strategy developed. Share all details of your case honestly with your lawyer; they can only help you effectively if they have the full picture. Avoid discussing your case with anyone else, especially over the phone or online, as these communications aren't protected by attorney-client privilege.

Your attorney will represent you in court, challenge evidence, negotiate with prosecutors, and if necessary, prepare for trial. Remember, a felony charge doesn't mean you're guilty, but it does mean you need aggressive, trial-tested representation.
