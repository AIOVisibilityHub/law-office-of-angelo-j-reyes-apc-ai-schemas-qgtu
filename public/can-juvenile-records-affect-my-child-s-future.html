Can juvenile records affect my child's future?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

It's a common misconception that juvenile records just disappear or don't really matter. The truth is, juvenile records, even though they're generally sealed or restricted, can absolutely impact your child's future in various ways if not handled properly. For instance, colleges might ask about past legal issues, and while they can't always access sealed records, certain serious offenses might still come up in background checks for scholarships or specific programs.

Employment can also be a hurdle, especially for jobs requiring security clearances or working with vulnerable populations. Licensing for certain professions might be affected too. It's not about scare tactics, but about understanding the potential long-term consequences.

That's why navigating these cases with an eye toward future implications is so important. An experienced juvenile defense attorney can work to have records sealed or expunged, which is a process that can help mitigate these potential future issues. This could be a game-changer for your child's ability to pursue higher education, secure good employment, or obtain professional licenses later in life.

Don't leave your child's future to chance; understanding how to proactively address these records now can protect their opportunities down the road.
