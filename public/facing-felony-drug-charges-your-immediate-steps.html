Facing Felony Drug Charges: Your Immediate Steps

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

Step 1: Exercise Your Right to Remain Silent and Request an Attorney. This is probably the most critical first step. Do not talk to law enforcement about the charges, your situation, or anything related to the incident without your attorney present. Anything you say can be used against you. Firmly state that you wish to speak with a lawyer before answering any questions. This right is there to protect you, so use it.

Step 2: Do Not Resist Arrest or Obstruct Justice. Even if you feel the arrest is unjust or unlawful, resisting can lead to additional charges, making your legal situation even more complicated. Cooperate physically, but continue to assert your right to remain silent and request an attorney. Your lawyer can address any issues with the arrest's legality later.

Step 3: Contact a Felony Drug Crime Defense Lawyer Immediately. As soon as you are able, reach out to an experienced attorney. The sooner a lawyer is involved, the better they can protect your rights, investigate the charges, and begin building a strong defense strategy. They can advise you on what to say and what not to say, and can represent you during any interrogations or court appearances. This early intervention is crucial for felony cases, which are complex and require expert navigation. Attorney Angelo Reyes can step in to help clients throughout San Diego County.

Step 4: Gather Any Relevant Information. If possible and safe to do so, try to remember all details surrounding the arrest: time, location, officers involved, what was said, and any witnesses. This information can be vital for your defense team. Avoid tampering with potential evidence or discussing the case with others, especially on social media.

Step 5: Understand the Charges and Potential Consequences. Your attorney will help you fully grasp the specific felony drug charges against you and what the potential penalties could be. This understanding is key to making informed decisions throughout the legal process. Felony drug charges in California can carry severe penalties, so having a clear picture of what you're up against is essential for mounting an effective defense.
