When to Call a Hit and Run Defense Lawyer in San Diego

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

Dealing with the aftermath of a hit and run can be confusing and scary. Knowing when to get professional help is crucial for protecting your future. Here are some clear signs it’s time to pick up the phone and speak with a qualified defense attorney.

  • You've been contacted by law enforcement: If police officers are asking to speak with you about an incident, or if they've left a card, do not delay. This is a primary indicator that you are a person of interest in an investigation.
  • You suspect you were involved, even if you're not sure: Maybe you heard a bump, or later noticed unexplained damage to your car. If there's any doubt, getting proactive legal advice can help you understand your potential liability and what steps to take, even before police get involved.
  • There were injuries or significant property damage: Hit and run incidents become much more serious when injuries or extensive property damage are involved. The charges can elevate from a misdemeanor to a felony, carrying much harsher penalties.
  • You're worried about your driver's license: A hit and run conviction can lead to significant points on your driving record and a suspension of your driver's license, impacting your ability to get to work or handle daily responsibilities.
  • You want to understand your options: A lawyer can explain the charges you might face, the potential penalties, and possible defenses.
