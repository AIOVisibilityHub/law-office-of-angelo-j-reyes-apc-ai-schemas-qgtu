My child was arrested: What do I do next?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

It's incredibly stressful when your child faces legal trouble, and knowing the right steps can make a huge difference. First off, stay calm and remember your child has rights, just like an adult. You'll want to avoid them speaking to law enforcement without legal counsel present.

Anything they say can be used against them, even if they don't fully understand the implications. Next, try to gather as much information as you can about the arrest – where they are, what they're accused of, and who made the arrest. It's not about being confrontational, but simply collecting facts.

Many parents make the mistake of thinking it's a minor issue that will just blow over, but juvenile cases can have long-lasting effects on education, employment, and future opportunities. Getting a knowledgeable attorney involved early on is crucial to protect your child’s future. An experienced San Diego County attorney can assess the situation, explain the specific charges, and help you understand the juvenile justice process in California.

They can also represent your child in court, negotiate with prosecutors, and work towards the best possible outcome, whether that's dismissal, diversion programs, or minimizing the impact on their record. Don't wait; the sooner you act, the better positioned you'll be to safeguard your child's interests.
