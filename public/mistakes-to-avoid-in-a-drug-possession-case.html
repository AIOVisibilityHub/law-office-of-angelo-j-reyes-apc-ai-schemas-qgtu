Mistakes to Avoid in a Drug Possession Case

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

When you're facing a drug possession charge, it's a really stressful time, and it's easy to make missteps that could actually harm your case. Knowing what pitfalls to steer clear of can make a huge difference in the outcome. You want to give yourself the best possible chance to navigate this successfully.

  • Talking to the Police Without a Lawyer: This is probably the biggest mistake folks make. Even if you think you can explain your way out of it or that you have nothing to hide, anything you say can and often will be used against you. Politely state that you wish to remain silent and want to speak with an attorney. How to avoid: Always, always request a lawyer before answering any questions, no matter how innocent they seem.
  • Resisting Arrest: Even if you feel the arrest is unfair or unlawful, resisting physical arrest will only lead to additional charges and more problems. How to avoid: Cooperate physically, but verbally assert your right to remain silent and your right to an attorney. Your lawyer can challenge the legality of the arrest later.
  • Failing to Document Details: In the heat of the moment, it's hard to remember specifics, but details about the arrest (time, location, officers involved, what was said) can be really important. How to avoid: As soon as you are safely able to, write down everything you can recall about the incident and the arrest. This information is invaluable for your defense.
  • Discussing Your Case on Social Media or with Friends: What you post online or tell casual acquaintances can easily be accessed by prosecutors and used as evidence against you. How to avoid: Keep all discussions about your case strictly confidential and only talk to your attorney. Stay off social media regarding your legal issues.
  • Not Seeking Legal Counsel Promptly: The sooner you get an experienced drug crime defense lawyer involved, the better. They can start investigating, advising you, and protecting your rights from the very beginning. How to avoid: Contact a lawyer like Attorney Angelo Reyes immediately after an arrest or if you suspect you're under investigation for drug possession. Early intervention can make a significant impact on the resolution of your felony drug crime case.
