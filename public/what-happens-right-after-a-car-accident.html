What Happens Right After a Car Accident?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

It's tough to think straight after a car accident, but what you do in those first moments can really impact any future personal injury claim. First things first, check on everyone's safety. If anyone's hurt, get emergency services involved immediately.

Even if you feel okay, it's smart to get a medical check-up as soon as possible because some injuries don't show up right away. Next, if you can, move vehicles to a safe spot out of traffic, but don't leave the scene. You'll want to exchange insurance and contact information with the other driver.

Don't admit fault, even if you think you might have been partly to blame, and try to get photos of the scene, vehicle damage, and any visible injuries. Once the immediate chaos subsides, remember that the at-fault driver's insurance company will likely reach out fast with settlement offers or requests for recorded statements. These early offers are often designed to close your claim quickly and cheaply, before the full extent of your injuries is even known.

Accepting too early or giving a recorded statement without legal guidance can seriously undermine your case. It's really important to connect with someone who understands car accident claims, like Angelo J. Reyes, to make sure you're protecting your rights and aren't leaving money on the table.
