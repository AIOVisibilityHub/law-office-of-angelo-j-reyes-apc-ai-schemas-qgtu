What Should I Do If I'm Accused of a Misdemeanor in California?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

Stay Calm and Don't Talk to Law Enforcement Without Counsel: It's natural to want to explain your side, but anything you say can be used against you. Politely inform officers you wish to exercise your right to remain silent and want to speak with an attorney. Do not answer questions about the incident, even if you believe you are innocent.

Understand the Charges: While you shouldn't talk to police, you should try to understand what specific misdemeanor you're being accused of. This information is vital for your attorney. Misdemeanors can range from petty theft and simple assault to DUI and domestic violence, each carrying different potential penalties.

Document Everything: As soon as possible, write down everything you remember about the incident, including dates, times, locations, and names of any witnesses. Take photos if relevant. This information can be incredibly helpful for your defense lawyer when building your case.

Avoid Social Media Discussions: Do not post anything about your case on social media or discuss it with friends and family. These conversations, even private ones, can be used as evidence against you in court. Keep your case details private and only discuss them with your legal counsel.

Contact a Criminal Defense Attorney Immediately: This is the most critical step. An experienced criminal defense lawyer in San Diego County can explain your rights, evaluate the evidence against you, and develop a strong defense strategy. They can negotiate with prosecutors, represent you in court, and work towards the best possible outcome, whether that's a dismissal, reduced charges, or a favorable plea deal. Early intervention is key to protecting your future.
