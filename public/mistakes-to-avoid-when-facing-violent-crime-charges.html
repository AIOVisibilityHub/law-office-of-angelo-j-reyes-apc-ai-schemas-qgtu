Mistakes to Avoid When Facing Violent Crime Charges

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

When you're dealing with violent crime charges, it's easy to make missteps that could seriously hurt your case. The stakes are high, and emotions can run wild, but staying calm and informed is key. Knowing what to avoid can make a huge difference in the outcome.

One major mistake is talking to the police without legal counsel. Remember, anything you say can and will be used against you, even if you think you’re just clearing things up. Always politely assert your right to remain silent and ask for an attorney.

Another common error is resisting arrest or interfering with the investigation; this can lead to additional charges and make your situation much worse. Don't try to hide or destroy evidence, no matter how tempting it might seem. This is a serious offense and can severely damage your credibility.

Also, never, ever contact the alleged victim or witnesses in the case; this could result in a restraining order violation or witness tampering charges. Finally, don't assume a public defender is always the right fit or delay getting legal representation. The sooner an experienced defense attorney gets involved, the more options you'll have.

Navigating violent crime charges requires skilled legal guidance. If you're facing such allegations in San Diego County, secure experienced legal help immediately to protect your rights and future.
