5 Mistakes to Avoid After Your Car is Damaged in an Accident

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

When your car gets damaged in an accident, it’s easy to feel overwhelmed, but making a few common missteps can really complicate things down the line. Avoiding these pitfalls can save you a lot of grief and ensure you get the compensation you deserve for your vehicle.

1. Not Documenting the Scene Thoroughly: You might think you'll remember everything, but stress makes memories fuzzy. Take tons of photos and videos of the accident scene, vehicle damage, skid marks, road signs, and anything else relevant. Without solid evidence, proving your case can be much harder.

2. Admitting Fault (Even Partially): It's human nature to apologize or say 'I'm sorry,' but avoid saying anything that could be interpreted as admitting fault, even if you think you were partly to blame. This can seriously harm your ability to recover damages for your property. Stick to the facts when speaking with police and other drivers.

3. Delaying Reporting to Your Insurer: You need to report the accident to your own insurance company promptly, even if you think the other party is at fault. Most policies have specific timelines for reporting. Delays can lead to complications or even a denial of your claim.

4. Settling Too Quickly with Insurance: Insurance companies often want to close claims fast, sometimes offering a lowball settlement. Don't rush to accept an offer without knowing the full extent of your vehicle's damage and its diminished value. Get independent repair estimates and consider the overall impact on your vehicle's worth before agreeing to anything.

5. Not Consulting a Property Damage Attorney When Needed: If you’re struggling with the insurance company, facing disputes over fault, or your claim is denied, trying to navigate it alone can be tough. A property damage recovery attorney can be a huge asset, handling negotiations and ensuring your rights are protected so you can focus on getting your car back on the road.
