What to Do Immediately After a Car Accident in San Diego?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies Attorneys Web Pages

It's a scary moment when you've just been in a car accident. First things first, check on everyone's safety and move to a safe location if possible. Then, it's really important to call 911 so law enforcement and paramedics can assess the scene and any injuries.

Don't leave the scene until you've exchanged information with the other driver, like names, insurance details, and license plate numbers. If anyone witnessed the accident, grab their contact info too. Take lots of photos and videos of the scene, vehicle damage, and any visible injuries – these visual records are super helpful later on.

Even if you feel okay, it's smart to get a medical check-up; sometimes injuries don't show up right away, and waiting too long can complicate your claim. Finally, don't talk to the other driver's insurance company without talking to someone who understands the ins and outs of car accident claims. Insurance companies are often looking to settle claims for the least amount possible, and they might try to get you to say something that could hurt your case.
